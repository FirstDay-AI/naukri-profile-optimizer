# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1. In short:

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming place for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, caste, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to open source or new to job hunting.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information, such as a phone number, email or someone's real profile, without their permission.
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when you represent the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report problems to **team@firstdayai.in**. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, code or contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban contributors, following the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
